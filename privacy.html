Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Who We Are

BTTR FIT is a fitness tracking application. Contact us at bttrfitness@gmail.com with any questions about this policy.

Information We Collect

Waitlist information: If you join our early-access waitlist, we collect your name, email address, state, and city so we can notify you at launch and understand where interest is coming from.

Account information: Email address and display name when you create an account, managed by Firebase Authentication (Google LLC).

Fitness data: Workout sessions (exercises, sets, weights, reps), running data (GPS route, distance, duration, pace), body measurements, and nutrition logs you enter.

Health data (with your permission): Step count, heart rate, active calories, body weight, and water intake read from Apple Health. Workout and running data written to Apple Health.

Technical data: Crash reports and performance data collected via Sentry (anonymized). Device type and iOS version for crash debugging only.

How We Use Your Information

  • To notify waitlist members when BTTR FIT launches
  • To provide and improve the app's fitness tracking features
  • To display your personal fitness statistics and progress
  • To sync your data across sessions
  • To identify and fix crashes and technical issues

HealthKit

BTTR FIT integrates with Apple HealthKit to read and write fitness data. We use HealthKit data solely to provide fitness tracking features within the app. We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or share it with third parties for purposes unrelated to health and fitness.

Third-Party Services

Firebase / Google LLC: Account management and cloud data storage. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Sentry: Anonymous crash reporting to identify technical issues. Privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy

MapLibre / Maptiler: Mapping services to display run routes. Privacy policy: maptiler.com/privacy-policy

Firebase Analytics: included as part of the Firebase SDK, but data collection is explicitly disabled in this app. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Google Ads, or any advertising SDK to collect data about you, track you across apps or websites, or serve you ads.

Waitlist / email provider: we use a third-party email service to store waitlist sign-ups and send launch notifications. We do not sell or share waitlist information with anyone else.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account. Waitlist information is retained until launch notifications are sent, or until you ask us to remove it.

Children's Privacy

BTTR FIT is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us at bttrfitness@gmail.com if you believe we have collected data from a child under 13.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data (including removal from the waitlist) by contacting us at bttrfitness@gmail.com or using the in-app account deletion and data export features.

If you are located in the European Economic Area or UK (GDPR): you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. Our legal basis for processing is your consent (given at signup) and our legitimate interest in operating the app's core features.

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this policy.
